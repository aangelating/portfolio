Hello, I'm

Frontend Developer, Web Designer, 2DAnimator, and based in Valencia,Spain.

I’m a multidisciplinary artist working across web design, 2D animation and illustration. I design and build websites that are clear, visual and full of personality, and I enjoy exploring different forms of visual expression.

I studied Fine Arts and completed a Master in Animation, focused on animation project direction, at the Polytechnic University of Valencia (UPV), and I’m currently studying Web Design at CEI. Since 2025 I’ve worked as a 2D animator and clean-up artist on projects such as League of Legends and Marvel Cosmic Invasion.

These experiences gave me a broad creative perspective and taught me to manage complex projects from the initial concept to the final result. I bring all of that into every interface I design.
